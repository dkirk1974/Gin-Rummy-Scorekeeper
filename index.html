Scoring Rules

  • Knocking: The knocker scores the difference if their deadwood is lower than the defender's.
  • Undercut: If the defender's deadwood is less than or equal to the knocker's deadwood, the defender wins the difference plus a 25-point bonus.
  • Gin: The player with Gin scores the defender's total deadwood plus a 25-point bonus.
