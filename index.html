Gin Rummy Scoring Rules

Gin: A player with zero deadwood scores the defender's deadwood plus a 25-point bonus.

Knocking: A player with 10 or fewer points of deadwood can knock. If their deadwood is lower than the defender's, they win the difference.

Undercut: If the defender's deadwood is less than or equal to the knocker's deadwood, the defender wins and scores the difference plus a 25-point undercut bonus.
