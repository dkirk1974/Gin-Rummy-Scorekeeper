1. Game Objective & Setup

The objective of Gin Rummy is to form matched sets/runs of cards and reduce unmatched cards ("deadwood") in your hand.

  • Deck: Standard 52-card deck (Aces are always low).
  • Deal: Each player is dealt 10 cards.
  • Piles: The remaining cards form the face-down Stock. The top card is turned face up to start the Discard pile.

2. Melds & Card Values

Melds are groups of valid card combinations. A card can only belong to one meld at a time.

  • Sets (Groups): 3 or 4 cards of the same rank (e.g., 8♠-8♥-8♦).
  • Runs (Sequences): 3 or more consecutive cards in the same suit (e.g., 4♣-5♣-6♣). Note: Aces are strictly low (A-2-3 is valid; K-A-2 is illegal).
Deadwood Card Values:
• Aces: 1 point
• Number Cards (2–10): Face value
• Face Cards (J, Q, K): 10 points each

3. Turn Gameplay

Players alternate turns taking the following actions in strict sequence:

  • 1. Draw: Take the top card from either the face-down Stock pile or the face-up Discard pile.
  • 2. Discard: Place 1 card face-up onto the Discard pile. (You cannot discard the exact card you just picked up from the discard pile on the same turn).

4. Knocking & Going Gin

You may end the hand on your turn after drawing and before discarding if your remaining deadwood meets the threshold:

  • Knocking: You can knock if your unmatched deadwood totals 10 points or less. You discard one card face-down and lay your melds on the table.
  • Going Gin: Knocking with 0 points of deadwood. You get a 25-point bonus, and the defender is blocked from laying off any cards.

5. Laying Off Cards

The Defender's Advantage: When a player knocks (not Gin), the defender gets to attach their own deadwood cards onto the knocker's melds to drop their count.
  • Extending Runs: Attaching a 6♥ or 10♥ to a knocker's 7♥-8♥-9♥ run.
  • Completing Sets: Attaching a fourth Jack to the knocker's three-Jack set.
  • Rule: Only the defender can lay off onto the knocker's cards. The knocker cannot lay off onto defender cards.

6. Scoring & Undercut Rules

After laying off, players tally remaining deadwood points:

Standard Knock: Knocker Deadwood < Defender Deadwood
Knocker Scores = Defender Deadwood - Knocker Deadwood
Undercut: Defender Deadwood ≤ Knocker Deadwood
Defender Scores = (Knocker Deadwood - Defender Deadwood) + 25 Bonus
*Note: Defender wins all ties automatically.
Going Gin: Knocker Deadwood = 0
Knocker Scores = Defender Deadwood + 25 Bonus (No Lay Offs)

7. Winning the Match

Hands are played sequentially until a player reaches the designated target score or players decide to finish an open-ended game.
